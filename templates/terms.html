1. Purpose of OrganLink

OrganLink is a digital platform designed to facilitate communication, registration, awareness, and information sharing between organ donors, recipients, hospitals, and healthcare professionals.

OrganLink does not perform organ transplantation, medical diagnosis, medical treatment, organ allocation, or compatibility verification.

2. Role of OrganLink

OrganLink acts solely as an intermediary technology platform. The platform provides tools for registration, document management, awareness resources, and communication between stakeholders.

All medical decisions, donor approvals, recipient approvals, compatibility assessments, and transplant procedures remain the sole responsibility of authorized hospitals and licensed medical professionals.

3. Registration and Verification Process

Creating an OrganLink account does not automatically qualify a user as a verified donor or verified recipient.

After registration, users may be required to upload supporting documents including, but not limited to, government-issued identification, blood group reports, medical fitness certificates, consent forms, medical reports, prescriptions, diagnostic reports, and official hospital documentation.

All uploaded documents are reviewed solely by authorized hospitals registered on the OrganLink platform.

OrganLink does not verify, authenticate, validate, or approve any documents uploaded by users.

To complete verification, donors and recipients may be required to appear in person at the hospital selected for verification.

Only after successful document review and in-person verification may a hospital mark a donor or recipient as "Verified."

4. No Medical Advice

Information provided through OrganLink is intended solely for educational and informational purposes.

Nothing on the platform should be interpreted as medical advice, diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or professional healthcare guidance.

5. AI Compatibility and Recommendations

Any compatibility scores, matching suggestions, rankings, recommendations, or AI-generated outputs displayed on OrganLink are provided solely for research, educational, and informational purposes.

Such results must not be used as the sole basis for any medical, legal, or transplant-related decision.

Final compatibility determination must always be conducted by authorized hospitals and qualified transplant specialists.
